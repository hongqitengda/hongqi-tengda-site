WHEN TO CUSTOMIZE

Which circumstances are more appropriate for methodological development

When an off-the-shelf project is unable to directly cover research issues, methodological feasibility and sample matrix assessment are undertaken before formal testing options are established.

NEW TARGET / COMPLEX MATRIX

New target or complex sample matrix

For example, emerging transformation products, complex environmental samples, special organisms or material matrices that require recertification for extraction, purification, separation and detection conditions.

RESEARCH-ORIENTED DESIGN

Scientific Quantitative and Comparative Design

When the project involves multiple processing groups, time series, standard curves, recovery rates, gaps and quality control, complete data links can be designed according to paper or subject needs.

METHOD DEVELOPMENT WORKFLOW

Methodological development and customization process

From the clarification of needs, the feasibility will be judged before the methodological conditions, quality control and formal sample analysis.

01

Research needs identified

Target object, sample type, sample volume, expected concentration and research purpose.

02

Route and matrix assessment

Identification of pre-treatment, analysis of platforms, jamming factors and feasibility.

03

Methodological conditions established

Optimizing extraction, purification, separation, testing and quantification conditions.

04

Quality control design

Set blanks, standard curves, markup or duplicate measurements according to project needs.

05

Formal testing and delivery

Sample analysis completed and results, graphs and descriptions organized for scientific purposes.
