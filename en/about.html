About the Company

Linked to scientific technology services and digital tools to provide more efficient project support to research and engineering teams.

Companies and brands

Shanghai Hongqi Tengda Information Technology Co., Ltd.Operation of the Scientific Technology Service and Digital Solutions Platform of the Red Tunta Science Service. The platform targets universities, research institutes and enterprise research and development teams, focusing on simulation of computing, material representation, data processing, scientific materials, software development and AI support tools.

Principles of service

  • Needs first:The objective of the study, the input material, the methodological boundary and the expected delivery are identified before the implementation programme is developed.
  • Process transparency:Description of key nodes, data sources, parameter settings and range of results to reduce information asymmetries.
  • Normative delivery:Delivery of raw data, charts, descriptions, reports, codes or system results by project type.
  • Compliance:In the case of third-party software, databases, experimental resources and branding material, the legal authorization and actual project requirements shall prevail.

Clients

Major services, research institutes, hospitals and engineering research and development teams, science and technology enterprises, and organizations that need tools to digitize scientific research.
