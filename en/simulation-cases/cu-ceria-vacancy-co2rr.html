Oxygen Vacancies in Cu/CeO₂ for CO₂ Electroreduction

What the simulations resolved: Intermediate free-energy profiles resolved how the *COOH-to-*CO step changes on Cu/CeO₂. Bader charges and Cu d-projected DOS explained how oxygen vacancies alter the electronic structure of the isolated Cu site.

Computational metricsOriginal paper DOI

Research question

Can a ceria oxygen vacancy both anchor an isolated Cu atom and alter CO₂ reduction intermediates?

Computational metrics: CO₂ reduction free energies and Cu d states

Reported simulation metrics

  • Adsorption energies and Gibbs free energies of key intermediates (Fig. 6)
  • Bader charge and charge-density differences
  • DOS and Cu d-projected density of states
