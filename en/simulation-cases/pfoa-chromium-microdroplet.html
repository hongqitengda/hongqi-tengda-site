PFOA and chromium(VI): how a microdroplet interface promotes coupled reactions

What the simulations resolved: MD and AIMD described PFOA enrichment and K⁺-mediated Cr(VI) colocalization at the interface. DFT quantified a representative α-C–F cleavage barrier of 2.80 eV in bulk versus 1.39 eV in the microdroplet model.

Computational metricsOriginal paper DOI

Research question

Why do PFOA and Cr(VI) promote each other's transformation at a microdroplet air–water interface rather than simply compete for reaction sites?

Computational metrics: Interfacial enrichment and C–F cleavage barriers

  • HOMO–LUMO and Fukui descriptors
