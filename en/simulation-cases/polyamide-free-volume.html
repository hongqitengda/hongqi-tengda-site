COMPUTATIONAL CASE · MD / DFT

Polyamide nanofiltration membranes: how monomer sterics open water pathways

What the simulations resolved: MD connected interfacial diffusion of piperazine-based monomers to membrane structure, while DFT compared polymerization free energies. Together with characterization, the calculations explained how methyl sterics affected chain packing and free-volume connectivity.

Computational metricsOriginal paper DOI

Research question

Can methyl substitution loosen polyamide chain packing to improve water transport while retaining the PFAS tested?

Computational metrics: Diffusion coefficients and interfacial binding free energy

Reported simulation metrics

  • Monomer mean square displacement (MSD) and diffusion coefficients
  • Number-density profiles and monomer distribution across the water/organic interface
  • Interfacial binding free energy and polymerization free energy (Fig. 4)

Experimental benchmark

one membrane achieved a measured water permeance of 57.8 ± 2.8 L·m⁻²·h⁻¹·bar⁻¹ and over 90% rejection for the PFAS tested.
